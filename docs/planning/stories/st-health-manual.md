Provide a manual history for body weight, hip, waist, neck, and high waist at belt height. Review how the history relates to the existing weight and height settings during design.

## Acceptance criteria

- Log weight by itself and record or correct each of the four circumference measurements independently.
- Verify that the saved date, unit, measurement site, and manual source are preserved on readback.
