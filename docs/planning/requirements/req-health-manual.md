title: "Log body weight and core circumferences manually"
---

Support dated manual entry of body weight and circumferences at the hip, waist, neck, and high waist at belt height. Manual body weight is a core workflow and can be logged on its own.

## Acceptance criteria

- Save, view, and correct each measurement independently with its date and unit. Standard waist and high waist at belt height remain separate measurement sites.
- Keep manual and Garmin weight readings distinguishable when both are present.
