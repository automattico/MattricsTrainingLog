title: "Calculate BMI and the hip/waist ratio"
---

Calculate BMI from body weight in kilograms and the profile height in meters squared. Track the requested hip/waist ratio from paired hip and standard-waist measurements. The proposed interpretation is hip divided by standard waist. Confirm the direction and display label during design.

## Acceptance criteria

- Verify both calculations with synthetic inputs. Show the measurement date and which inputs were used. Apply the designed pairing rule to hip and standard waist.
- Show when required inputs are missing or invalid. Keep high-waist measurements available as their own series.
