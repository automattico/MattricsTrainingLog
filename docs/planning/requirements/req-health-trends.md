title: "Review health trends with time filters"
---

Provide a dedicated Health Data section with charts for tracked readings and derived metrics over time.

## Acceptance criteria

- Changing the selected time range updates the charts. Each series identifies its metric, unit, and source where applicable.
- Show gaps as missing readings. Make chart dates and values available without relying on color or hover alone.
